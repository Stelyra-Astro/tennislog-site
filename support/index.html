Help & support

A little help, so you can get back on court.

The iPhone app is preparing for App Store release. The interactive website is a separate browser demo; it never starts a purchase.

Email support ↗Open the demo

Start with the essentials

Log a session with its time and activity. Add people, a court or equipment as you go. Use Repeat last setup to reuse a familiar arrangement; previous scores and consumable replacements should not carry over.

My profile, levels and achievements

Open Me to edit your private profile. Keep dated levels under My level and your own milestones under My achievements. Other people's results belong to their People records. Self-assessed ratings are not verified federation ratings.

Back up your records

  1. In Me, choose Export complete backup.
  2. Keep the JSON file somewhere you can find again, such as Files or iCloud Drive.
  3. To recover, choose Restore a backup and select the appropriate file. Read the replacement confirmation before continuing.

The web demo and native app currently have different backup formats. A demo backup is not an iPhone backup. Browser storage may be removed by private browsing, device cleanup or clearing website data. Automatic iCloud synchronization is not enabled.

Inventory and string setups

Record purchases, usage and stock corrections on the correct date. Counts and usage are recalculated from history. In the native app, string quantities use metres and ball quantities use individual balls. Confirm the unit in the relevant editor before saving.

Subscription help

The US plans are $5.99 per month or $59.99 per year. Eligible first-time subscribers may receive a 14-day monthly trial or a one-month annual trial after selecting a plan and confirming with Apple. Apple shows the final price and eligibility before purchase. Canceling renewal during a trial keeps access until the current trial period ends.

For an existing Apple subscription, use Manage subscriptions. For Apple billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support. Deleting an app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Report a problem

Email Stelyra-Astro@proton.me with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot with personal information removed can help. Do not send passwords, payment information or your full backup unless we specifically discuss why it is necessary.

Reset the website demo

Use Reset demo on the marketing page or in the demo's Me screen. This clears only this TennisLog demo's browser records. It does not change iPhone records or cancel subscriptions.
