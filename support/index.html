Help & support

Updated November 27, 2026

Help for the native iPhone app and the separate browser demo.

TennisLog 1.0 is the current iPhone release. This website's demo uses sample data in your browser; it does not connect to your iPhone or start a purchase.

Email support ↗Open the demo

Record a session or tournament

Use the add button in Home or Sessions to record play or plan a future session. For a match, add players and scores; you can also attach people, court, equipment and notes.

Keep a separate backup

TennisLog 1.0 keeps records on your iPhone and does not automatically sync them between devices. Export a copy of important records to Files. The browser demo has its own separate storage.

Export or restore a backup

  1. Open Me in the iPhone app and choose Export complete backup.
  2. Save the file somewhere you control.
  3. To restore, choose Restore a backup and review the replacement confirmation.

Website demo backups use a different format and cannot be imported into the iPhone app.

Membership and purchases

Monthly and yearly plans unlock adding and editing. The configured US prices are $5.99 per month and $59.99 per year. Apple decides whether you qualify for the 14-day monthly or one-month annual introductory trial and displays the final local terms before confirmation. If access ends, your existing records stay readable and exportable.

Use Me → TennisLog membership → Restore purchases for an existing purchase. You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription and request help with Apple billing or refunds through Apple. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

Report a problem

Email Stelyra-Astro@proton.me with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, the screen and steps involved, and any error text. A screenshot with private information removed can help. Do not send passwords, payment details or a full backup unless we agree it is needed.

Reset the website demo

Use Reset demo on the marketing page or in the demo's Me screen. This clears only this site's browser data. It does not affect iPhone records, exported backups or a subscription.
