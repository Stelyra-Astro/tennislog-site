Help & support

Updated November 25, 2026

Help for the native iPhone app and the separate browser demo.

TennisLog is an iPhone app in release preparation. This website's demo uses sample data in your browser; it does not connect to your iPhone or start a purchase.

Email support ↗Open the demo

Record a session or tournament

Use the add button in Home or Sessions to open the shared Log session / Schedule form. A future session can be planned, then confirmed after you play. For a match, add players and sets; for a tournament, select or create its reusable Library entry, then record this edition and its individual matches. Only matches you played count toward your personal session totals.

Automatic iCloud sync

On iPhone, open Me → Sync & backup. Automatic iCloud sync is on by default and can be switched off. When enabled, TennisLog keeps a local copy and merges records through your private iCloud database on iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. Use Sync now to request an update. An internet connection, available iCloud storage and iCloud access for TennisLog are required. If a sync fails, your local records remain on the device; check the status shown in the app and try again later.

Cross-device editing and the production-signed sync flow are still being verified before release. Keep a separate Files backup of important records. The browser demo has no iCloud connection.

Export or restore a manual backup

  1. Open Me → Sync & backup → Manual backup.
  2. Export a complete copy to Files and keep it somewhere you control.
  3. To import, select a TennisLog iPhone backup and review the replacement summary before confirming.

An imported backup replaces the current local records; it is not the same as automatic iCloud merging. Website demo backups use a different format and cannot be imported into the iPhone app.

Language and appearance

Choose Me → Data & settings → Language for Follow system, English or 简体中文. TennisLog keeps its dark appearance under either iPhone system appearance. The browser demo currently uses English.

Membership and purchases

Monthly and yearly plans unlock adding and editing. The configured US prices are $5.99 per month and $59.99 per year. Apple decides whether you qualify for the 14-day monthly or one-month annual introductory trial and displays the final local terms before confirmation. If access ends, your existing records stay readable and exportable.

Use Me → TennisLog membership → Restore purchases for an existing purchase. You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription and request help with Apple billing or refunds through Apple. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

Report a problem

Email Stelyra-Astro@proton.me with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, the screen and steps involved, and any error text. A screenshot with private information removed can help. Do not send passwords, payment details or a full backup unless we agree it is needed.

Reset the website demo

Use Reset demo on the marketing page or in the demo's Me screen. This clears only this site's browser data. It does not affect iPhone records, iCloud records or a subscription.
