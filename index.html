A few things to know

Before your first hit.

Do I need an account?

No TennisLog account is required. TennisLog 1.0 stores records on your iPhone. Apple handles subscriptions; Restore purchases is available in the app.

Is this an online club or booking service?

No. People and court records are your private notes. TennisLog does not contact players, book courts or publish a social profile.

Can I keep a backup?

Yes. In the iPhone app, use Me to export a complete backup to Files or restore one. Records do not automatically sync between devices in version 1.0. The browser demo can export its own JSON file; demo and iPhone backup formats are different.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

TennisLog is iPhone only. There is no Watch app or sensor analysis.

Where can I get help?

Visit our support page or email Stelyra-Astro@proton.me. Please don't send private records unless they're needed to explain your issue.
