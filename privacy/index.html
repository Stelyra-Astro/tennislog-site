Privacy policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy describes TennisLog, its development versions, and this website, operated under the Stelyra Astro name. Contact Stelyra-Astro@proton.me about privacy questions.

TennisLog for iPhone is preparing for App Store release. The website offers a separate interactive browser demo. The iPhone app uses Apple in-app purchases and RevenueCat for subscription access; it does not sync tennis records to a cloud account.

Your tennis records

The native app stores the profile, sessions, people, courts, equipment, expenses, plans, notes and photos you enter in its local app storage. You do not create a TennisLog account. We do not operate a server that receives these tennis records. A person you add to your private records is not contacted or given a public profile.

Photos are selected using the system photo picker. Only the items you choose are used. Local notifications are optional and require permission; reminder content can appear on your device's lock screen according to your system settings. This version does not access Apple Health, Watch sensors, contacts or precise device location.

Backups and sharing

Exporting a backup creates a file containing your records and included photos. The destination you select, such as a cloud drive or another app, then handles that file under its own policies. System device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Sharing a summary similarly sends only the content you choose to its selected destination. Keep backups private; they may contain information about you and other people.

Website and interactive demo

The demo stores its sample records and your edits in this browser's local storage. Demo data is separate from the iPhone app. We recommend using fictional details in the demo. Reset demo clears this demo's stored records; clearing website data in your browser is another option. The site does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or third-party fonts.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes even if they are not signed in. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links open services with their own data practices.

Subscriptions

The iPhone app uses Apple in-app purchases and RevenueCat to check subscription access and restore purchases without a TennisLog login. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription status, and technical information needed to provide and troubleshoot this service. Tennis records, notes, photos and backups are not sent to RevenueCat for this purpose. Payment credentials are handled by Apple, not entered into TennisLog. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. You can request help with subscription-related data at Stelyra-Astro@proton.me.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and attachments. We use them to respond and resolve the issue. Our support mailbox is hosted by Proton. Support correspondence is kept only as needed for support, related records and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence by email, subject to any retention that is legally required.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your local records, export a copy, control photo selection and notifications, and clear app or website data. Deleting local app data does not delete copies you exported or cloud/device backups managed by another service. Contact us about access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights relating to information we hold, such as support emails. We cannot retrieve local tennis records that have never been sent to us.

Updates

We will revise this page when actual data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies this version.
