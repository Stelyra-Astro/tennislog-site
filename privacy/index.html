Privacy policy

Updated November 25, 2026

This policy describes TennisLog for iPhone and the separate website demo operated under the Stelyra Astro name. For privacy questions, email Stelyra-Astro@proton.me.

Your tennis records are saved locally on your iPhone. Automatic iCloud sync is on by default in the current app and can be turned off. When enabled, records are stored and merged in your private iCloud database through Apple. The website demo is separate browser data.

Records you enter

The app can hold your profile, sessions, scores, people, courts, tournaments, equipment, stock, expenses, plans, notes and photos. People you add are private entries; TennisLog does not contact them or create public profiles. You do not need a TennisLog login. We do not run a TennisLog server that receives your tennis records.

You choose photos from the system photo picker or camera. Local reminders require permission and may appear on your lock screen according to your iPhone settings. TennisLog does not use Apple Health or Watch sensor data. Searching for a court may use a map or search provider; the app does not require continuous location tracking.

iCloud sync and manual backups

Automatic sync is enabled by default. When it is on and your iPhone has iCloud access, TennisLog exchanges records with your private iCloud database so devices using the same Apple Account can merge changes, including deletions. Device preferences, such as your app language choice, stay on the device. You can turn sync off in Me → Sync & backup; this stops future exchanges from that device but does not itself erase records already stored locally or in iCloud. Apple provides the iCloud service under its own terms and privacy policy.

You can separately export a complete backup to Files or import a backup after reviewing its replacement summary. The destination you choose for an exported file, such as iCloud Drive or another app, handles it under its own policies. An exported backup may include records and photos about you and other people; store it carefully. System device backups may also include app data depending on your Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple handles payment credentials and the subscription transaction. RevenueCat checks access and restoration without a TennisLog login. It may receive an anonymous app user identifier, purchase status and technical information needed for that service; tennis records, notes, photos and backups are not sent to RevenueCat for purchase verification. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Contact us for help with subscription-related data; Apple handles billing and refunds.

Website demo

Sample records and edits in the interactive demo are stored in this browser's local storage. They do not sync with the iPhone app or iCloud. Use fictional details. Reset demo removes only this demo's stored data; clearing browser site data is another option. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or third-party fonts to this site.

GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security even when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links lead to services with their own practices.

Support and your choices

If you email support, we receive your address, message and attachments to respond and resolve the issue. Our mailbox is hosted by Proton. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. You can edit and export app records, control sync, photo access and notifications, and remove browser demo data. Deleting local app data does not automatically erase exported files or records held in your private iCloud database. Contact us about access, correction or deletion rights relating to information we hold, such as support messages.

Changes

We will update this page when data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
