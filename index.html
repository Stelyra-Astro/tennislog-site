A few things to know

Before your first hit.

Do I need an account?

No TennisLog account is required. Your tennis records are stored locally. The iPhone subscription uses your Apple purchase account, with a Restore Purchases option.

Is this an online club or booking service?

No. People and court records are your private notes. TennisLog does not contact players, book courts or publish a social profile.

Can I keep a backup?

The demo lets you export its own JSON backup from Me. The iPhone app uses a separate native backup format. The two formats are not currently interchangeable. Keep copies of important records before clearing browser storage or deleting an app.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

The first release is for iPhone only. Watch workout demonstrations and simulated sensor analysis are outside the first release.

Where can I get help?

Visit our support page or email Stelyra-Astro@proton.me. Please don't send private records unless they're needed to explain your issue.
